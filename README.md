### Install on Android

[**Download EndraSpell Preview Build 27 (APK)**](https://github.com/brandonendall/EndraSpell/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/EndraSpell-Preview-Build27.apk)

Download the APK on your Android device, open it, and allow installation from your browser if Android asks. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

This is the exact previously delivered Build 27 APK, published without rebuilding or modifying it. It installs as **EndraSpell Preview**, separately from the older EndraSpell app. Keep the older app to retain its existing learner history and recordings; they do not transfer automatically to Preview.

The approved build includes the forest-and-gold theme, learner selection and management, visible Automatic voice / My recordings choices, and Back navigation to the selected learner's activity menu.
