### Install or update

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Download the APK on your Android device, open it, and allow installation from your browser if Android asks.

**Install directly over Build 28. Do not uninstall.** The app identity and signing key match Build 28, so Android can retain its learner names, word lists, recordings, and history during the update. Older Build 27 used a different app identity and does not transfer its data automatically.

### Learners

New installs begin with one editable **Learner 1**. Use **Add Learner** and **Remove Learner** on the learner-selection page. Rename learners through the home menu's **Edit Learners** option. Removing a learner archives their words and history; restore them from **Archived**.
